    GHGT-18 · Perth 2026

    Capture City 2050

    You run the power company of a coal-and-gas town. Keep the lights on until December 2050.

    • CO₂ limit. The bar shows how much CO₂ you emit per year; the green box is the legal limit, and it shrinks every year toward net zero. Every tonne above it adds to your CO₂ debt. A full debt bar ends the game.
    • Public anger rises with high prices, blackouts and smog. At 100 % you are voted out.
    • Your funds come from electricity bills. Below −$500M you go bankrupt.
    • Tap a plant to add CO₂ capture. Capture costs money and steam, so plants deliver less power. 90 % capture will not be enough by the 2040s.
    • The research lab unlocks NTHU solvents, one project at a time. New solvents can fail during their first 2 years of operation.
    • Some events ask you to decide. The game pauses until you choose.
