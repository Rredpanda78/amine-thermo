    GHGT-18 · Perth 2026

    Capture City 2050

    You run the power company of a coal-and-gas town. Keep the lights on until December 2050.

    • CO₂ limit. Keep the orange bar inside the green box. The box shrinks every year toward net zero. Going over fills the limit breach meter; at 100 % you lose your licence.
    • Public anger rises with high prices, blackouts and smog. At 100 % you are voted out.
    • Funds come from electricity bills. Below −$500M you go bankrupt.
    • Tap a plant to add CO₂ capture, upgrade it to 99 %, switch coal to gas, or demolish it. Tap + to build a new one.
    • The research lab unlocks new solvents. New solvents can fail in their first 2 years.
