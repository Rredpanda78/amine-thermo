    GHGT-18 · Perth 2026

    Capture City 2050

    You run the power company of a coal-and-gas town. Keep the lights on until December 2050.

    • CO₂ limit. Keep the orange bar inside the green box. The box shrinks every year toward net zero. Going over fills the limit breach meter; at 100 % you lose your licence.
    • Public anger rises with high prices, blackouts and smog. At 100 % you are voted out.
    • Funds come from electricity bills. Spare power sells to industry at a lower price, but dirty power stops paying once carbon is priced. Below −$500M you go bankrupt.
    • Tap a plant in the town to command it: add CO₂ capture, upgrade to 99 %, switch coal to gas, or demolish. Jobs queue up like in a strategy game: paid when queued, fully refunded if you cancel.
    • The research lab finds new solvents by slow, reliable experiments or fast, riskier computer screening. New solvents can fail in their first 2 years.

    Where the numbers come from. Capture technologies use values reported in Lin Research Group papers (Chem. Eng. J. 2025, 2026; Ind. Eng. Chem. Res. 2025; Sep. Purif. Technol. 2025) and pilot-plant literature. Values marked est. are game estimates. Plants, prices, the CO₂ limit, events and regions are simplified for play and are not forecasts.
